# Burhani Enterprise — Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity & Design Philosophy
Burhani Enterprise embodies **Premium Dark Luxury** tailored for modern industrial and commercial excellence. The design language communicates authority, precision craftsmanship, and high-trust reliability.

### Core Brand Principles:
- **Quiet Sophistication:** Deep, dark, atmospheric backgrounds paired with restrained metallic gold accents.
- **Precision Engineering:** Clean geometric grids, sharp hairline borders, and balanced whitespace.
- **Authenticity:** Genuine representation of products and materials without artificial embellishment.

---

## 2. Color Palette & Tokens

### Primary Dark / Navy Spectrum
- **Deep Obsidian (`--color-navy-950`):** `#030712` — Primary page canvas.
- **Midnight Navy (`--color-navy-900`):** `#0A1128` — Elevated section backgrounds and card headers.
- **Deep Navy (`--color-navy-800`):** `#001F54` — Card backgrounds, containers, and secondary layers.
- **Accent Navy (`--color-navy-700`):** `#0A2558` — Hover surfaces and dark badge fills.
- **Active Navy (`--color-navy-600`):** `#123472` — Interactive element backgrounds.

### Metallic Gold Spectrum
- **Champagne Light (`--color-gold-300`):** `#F5E6BE` — Highlight glints, secondary badges, text highlights.
- **Warm Gold (`--color-gold-400`):** `#E6CA65` — Interactive hover states, secondary icons.
- **Primary Imperial Gold (`--color-gold-500`):** `#D4AF37` — Primary brand color, CTA buttons, active accents.
- **Deep Metallic Gold (`--color-gold-600`):** `#AA7C11` — Button borders, active states, key lines.
- **Antique Gold (`--color-gold-700`):** `#85610D` — Subtle borders and deep shadows.

### Contrast & Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)
- Body text on dark navy canvas must achieve a minimum contrast ratio of **4.5:1** (`#F8FAFC` or `#E2E8F0` on `#030712`).
- Headings and large text must achieve at least **3:1** against the background.
- Gold button text must use dark navy (`#030712` on `#D4AF37`) for high legibility and contrast compliance.

---

## 3. Typography System

### Primary Headlines: Elegant Serif
- **Family:** `Cinzel`, `Playfair Display`, Georgia, serif
- **Role:** Hero titles, section headers, brand wordmark.
- **Characteristics:** Letter-spaced (`tracking-wider` or `tracking-widest`), dignified, timeless.

### Body & Data: Precision Sans-Serif
- **Family:** `Inter`, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, sans-serif
- **Role:** Navigation links, paragraphs, product specifications, tabular data.
- **Characteristics:** High x-height, clear numeric tabular lining, legible at small scales.

---

## 4. Strict Asset & Imagery Rules

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Enforced Imagery Directives:**
> 1. **No Founder Photos:** Never display photos of company founders, executives, or personal team portraits.
> 2. **No Logo Until Provided:** Do not create or import temporary, fictional, or generic logo graphics. The brand identity must be expressed exclusively through a clean typographic wordmark (e.g. `BURHANI ENTERPRISE` in elegant serif tracking) until official vector brand assets are provided.
> 3. **Real Product Photography Only:** Every product showcase must utilize real, authentic photography of industrial goods and hardware. Generic 3D placeholders or unrelated consumer stock photography are strictly forbidden.

---

## 5. UI Accents & Micro-Interactions
- **Borders:** 1px hairline borders using gold with low opacity (`border-gold-500/20` to `border-gold-500/40`).
- **Cards:** Subtle drop shadows with dark ambient glow (`shadow-[0_4px_20px_-4px_rgba(212,175,55,0.08)]`).
- **Focus Rings:** High-visibility gold outline (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-gold-400 focus-visible:outline-none`).
